How it works

  1. Drop your chant audio (or the video itself). It is analysed right here on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.
  2. Detect chants. Set how many jaaps (108) and, if there is an intro or outro, where the chanting starts and ends. The detector learns what one repetition sounds like and places exactly that many markers.
  3. Check the timeline. Orange markers are less certain. Press , / . to hop between markers, drag to fix, double-click to add, ⌫ to delete. If a recording is tricky, use Tap along and press Space at every chant, then Snap to tighten the taps.
  4. Style it: mantra text, mala type, Devanagari numbers, still or turning mala.
  5. Export MP4 and drop it on a track above your video at 0:00 in Final Cut / iMovie / Premiere / DaVinci. Apply a green-screen (chroma) key, scale it down to a corner.

Your markers are remembered per audio file, and your style is remembered for next time. Use Save project to keep a copy you can reopen.
